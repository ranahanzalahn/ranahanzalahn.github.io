Things I've built

Loom Messenger

A messenger app built with Flutter and Dart.

Loom does what a messenger does, and it also translates calls on its own. I built a live Urdu-to-English translation pipeline into its voice and video calls, so someone speaking Urdu can be understood by someone who only speaks English.

Open Loom at shopbolt.pk

How the translation works

  1. CaptureThe audio from the call is picked up as someone speaks.
  2. TranscribeDeepgram Nova-3 turns the Urdu speech into text.
  3. TranslateGroq, running Llama 3.1, translates the text into English. I used it for its low latency.
  4. DeliverThe English translation reaches the other person while the call is still going.

On a live call a late translation is no use, so the whole pipeline is built around keeping each step fast.
